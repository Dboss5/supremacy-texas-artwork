## Rules and provenance

The review followed [COLLECTION-RULES.md](COLLECTION-RULES.md): exclude visible people and body parts, watermarks, prominent external source logos, promotional graphics, unrelated imagery, GIFs, Lottie animations, video and video posters. Source branding was not cropped out or erased to rescue rejected candidates. Uncertain candidates and their reasons remain in the review ledger.

Each accepted catalog entry has nonempty, plain-text title, alt text, caption and description. Source reference numbers, filename hashes and supplier boilerplate stay out of those public fields. Original source names, URLs, labels, retrieval dates, authorization evidence and hashes remain in provenance. Embedded source metadata was removed after recording that provenance, with orientation and cleanup method documented for every asset.

The user supplied the source list and directed continuation of the established collection workflow. No source-specific license document was supplied or independently verified. This record preserves the authorization actually available without treating public availability as a license or describing third-party imagery as a verified Supremacy job.
